# Project Statement

## Project Title
The Mystery Files: The Stolen Exam Paper

## Problem Statement
A sealed Calculus exam paper goes missing from a faculty office. The player must investigate the incident by examining the area, interviewing four people who were nearby, collecting evidence, and comparing the timeline before making an accusation.

## Scope
The project is a terminal-based, single-player detective game. It supports viewing suspects, investigating locations, collecting clues, interviewing suspects, reviewing evidence, viewing a timeline, and making a final accusation. It does not use a database, network connection, or external Python package.

## Target Users
Students and casual players who enjoy short, choice-based mystery games.

## High-Level Features
- Case introduction and suspect list
- Location-based investigation
- Clue collection with duplicate prevention
- Suspect interviews
- Evidence review and timeline
- Investigation progress summary
- Final accusation and case outcome
